## Decisions Made

- **Additive schema.** Multi-hop rows require `source_id`, `question`, `answer`, and
  `bridging_passages`; the original single-`evidence_quote` loader remains untouched.
- **Ordered values grouped by arm.** `summarize_multihop_results` accepts each arm's ordered
  per-question set-recall values and compares them with the named baseline arm.
- **Separate meanings, separate functions.** The D-07 rejection filter never delegates to the
  D-08 bridging-set metric.
- **Measurement before judgment.** This module computes no verdict. Plan 15 composes and
  mechanically verifies the 30-50 questions and captures the three-arm measurements; plan 16
  applies D-15's four conditions and D-16's disposition.
